# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when needed, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information and handle hardware-safety reports carefully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or sexualized attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Knowingly encouraging unsafe hardware behavior or concealing relevant risk.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Reports can be made
privately through the repository's GitHub contact and security options.
Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
